# Exercise 4. Dot Product and Perpendicularity

## Question

For $u=(1,2,-1)$ and $v=(2,-1,3)$ compute $u\cdot v$ and determine whether the vectors are perpendicular. Then find $a$ so that $w=(2,-1,a)$ is perpendicular to $u$, and check the result.

## Solution

Two vectors are perpendicular when their dot product is 0.

$$
u\cdot v=1\cdot2+2\cdot(-1)+(-1)\cdot3=2-2-3=-3
$$

It is not 0, so $u$ and $v$ are not perpendicular.

For $w=(2,-1,a)$ I need $u\cdot w=0$:

$$
u\cdot w=2-2-a=-a=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad a=0
$$

Check: for $w=(2,-1,0)$ we get $u\cdot w=2-2+0=0$, so $w$ is perpendicular to $u$.
