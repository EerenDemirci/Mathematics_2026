# Exercise 9

**Answer:** area $=11$.

**Steps**

$$
\det(u\ \ v)=\det
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & 1 \\
1 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix}=12-1=11,\qquad \det(v\ \ u)=\det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 3 \\
4 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}=1-12=-11
$$

**Rule:** area $=|\det|$. Swapping the vectors changes only the sign (orientation), not the area.

**Check:** $|u|^2|v|^2-(u\cdot v)^2=10\cdot17-7^2=121=11^2$.
