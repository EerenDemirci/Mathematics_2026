# Exercise 2

**Answer:** $\det A=33$.

**Steps (Sarrus)**

$$
(1\cdot3\cdot5+2\cdot4\cdot2+(-1)\cdot0\cdot1)-((-1)\cdot3\cdot2+1\cdot4\cdot1+2\cdot0\cdot5)=31-(-2)=33
$$

**Rule:** Sarrus: sum of the 3 "down" diagonals minus sum of the 3 "up" diagonals.

**Check:** $R_3\leftarrow R_3-2R_1$ does not change $\det$ and gives row $(0,-3,7)$; then $\det A=1\cdot(3\cdot7-4\cdot(-3))=33$.
