# Exercise 8

**Answer**

$$
\det(AB)=3\cdot(-5)=-15,\quad \det(BA)=(-5)\cdot3=-15,\quad \det(A^{-1}B)=\frac13\cdot(-5)=-\frac53
$$

**Rules:** $\det(AB)=\det A\det B$; $\det(A^{-1})=\frac{1}{\det A}$ ($A^{-1}$ exists since $\det A\neq0$).

**Why equal:** $\det A$ and $\det B$ are numbers, and numbers commute, even if $AB\neq BA$.

**Check:** $A=\mathrm{diag}(3,1)$, $B=\mathrm{diag}(-5,1)$ give $\det(AB)=-15$.
