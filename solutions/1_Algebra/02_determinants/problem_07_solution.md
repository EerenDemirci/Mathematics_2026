# Exercise 7

**Answer** ($n=3$, $\det A=-4$)

$$
\det(2A)=2^3\cdot(-4)=-32,\quad \det(A^T)=-4,\quad \det(A^2)=(-4)^2=16,\quad \det(-A)=(-1)^3\cdot(-4)=4
$$

**Rules:** $\det(cA)=c^n\det A$; $\det(A^T)=\det A$; $\det(AB)=\det A\det B$.

**Check:** with $A=\mathrm{diag}(-4,1,1)$: $2A=\mathrm{diag}(-8,2,2)$ has $\det=-32$.
