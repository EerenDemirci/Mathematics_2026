# Exercise 3

**Answer:** $\det A=6$, $\det B=-6$.

**Steps**

$$
\det A=(3+4+0)-(0+1+0)=6
$$

$B$ = $A$ with rows 1 and 2 swapped:

$$
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
0 & 3 & 1 \\
1 & 2 & 0 \\
2 & 1 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Prediction:** a row swap changes the sign, so $\det B=-6$.

**Check (calculation):**

$$
\det B=(0+0+1)-(4+0+3)=-6
$$
