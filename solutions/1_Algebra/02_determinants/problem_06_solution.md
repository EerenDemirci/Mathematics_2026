# Exercise 6

**Answer:** not invertible for $t=\sqrt2$ and $t=-\sqrt2$.

**Steps**

$$
\det A(t)=t\cdot t-1\cdot2=t^2-2=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=\pm\sqrt2
$$

**Rule:** not invertible $\Longleftrightarrow$ $\det=0$.

**Check:** $t=\sqrt2$: $\sqrt2\cdot(\sqrt2,1)=(2,\sqrt2)$ is the second row, so the rows are dependent.
