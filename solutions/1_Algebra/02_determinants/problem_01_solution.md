# Exercise 1. Determinant of a 2×2 Matrix

## Question

Compute

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 \\
5 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad \det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 7 \\
2 & 14 \\
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

Which matrix is invertible? Justify your answer.

## Solution

$$
\det
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & -2 \\
5 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix}=12+10=22,\qquad \det
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 7 \\
2 & 14 \\
\end{pmatrix}=14-14=0
$$

A matrix is invertible only if its determinant is not 0. So the first matrix is invertible and the second is not (its second row is 2 times the first row).
