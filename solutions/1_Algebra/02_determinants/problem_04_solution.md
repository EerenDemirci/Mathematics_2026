# Exercise 4

**Answer**

$$
\det=2\cdot(-3)\cdot7=-42
$$

**Rule:** the determinant of a triangular matrix is the product of the diagonal entries.

**Why:** expanding along the first column leaves only

$$
2\cdot\det
\begin{pmatrix}
-3 & 5 \\
0 & 7 \\
\end{pmatrix}=2\cdot(-3)\cdot7
$$

because all other terms contain a zero.

**Check:** in Sarrus' rule every product except the main diagonal contains a $0$.
