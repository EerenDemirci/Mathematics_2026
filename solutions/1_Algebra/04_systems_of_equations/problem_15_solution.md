# Exercise 15. Classifying Three Systems

## Question

For each of the following systems, perform only as much elimination as necessary to recognize its type:

**System 1**

$$
\begin{cases}
x+y=2, \\
x-y=0,
\end{cases}
$$

**System 2**

$$
\begin{cases}
x+y=2, \\
2x+2y=4,
\end{cases}
$$

**System 3**

$$
\begin{cases}
x+y=2, \\
2x+2y=5.
\end{cases}
$$

For each, justify the classification: one solution, infinitely many solutions, or no solution.

## Solution

System 1: $R_2\leftarrow R_2-R_1$ gives $-2y=-2$. So $y=1$, $x=1$, and there is one solution.

System 2: $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$ gives $0=0$. Only one equation is left, so there are infinitely many solutions.

System 3: $R_2\leftarrow R_2-2R_1$ gives $0=1$. This is impossible, so there is no solution.
