# Exercise 9. A System from a Sum Condition

## Question

Find numbers $x,y$ satisfying

$$
x+y=10,\qquad 2x+5y=32.
$$

Solve the system and explain geometrically why the solution is unique.

## Solution

From $x+y=10$ I get $2x+2y=20$. I subtract this from $2x+5y=32$:

$$
3y=12\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=4,\qquad x=6
$$

Each equation is a line. The slopes are $-1$ and $-\frac25$, so the lines are not parallel and they cross in only one point. That is why the solution is unique.
