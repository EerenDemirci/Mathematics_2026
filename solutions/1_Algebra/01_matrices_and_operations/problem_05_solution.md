# Exercise 5

**Answer**

$$
Ax=
\begin{pmatrix}
2\cdot4-1\cdot2 \\
1\cdot4+3\cdot2 \\
\end{pmatrix}=
\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**As a combination of columns:**

$$
4
\begin{pmatrix}
2 \\
1 \\
\end{pmatrix}+2
\begin{pmatrix}
-1 \\
3 \\
\end{pmatrix}=
\begin{pmatrix}
8 \\
4 \\
\end{pmatrix}+
\begin{pmatrix}
-2 \\
6 \\
\end{pmatrix}=
\begin{pmatrix}
6 \\
10 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Rule:** $Ax=x_1\cdot(\mathrm{column\ 1})+x_2\cdot(\mathrm{column\ 2})$.

**Check:** both methods give $(6,10)^T$.
