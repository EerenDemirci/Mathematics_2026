# Exercise 9

**Answer**

$$
Sx=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 \\
2 \\
\end{pmatrix},\quad RSx=
\begin{pmatrix}
-2 \\
2 \\
\end{pmatrix};\qquad Rx=
\begin{pmatrix}
-2 \\
1 \\
\end{pmatrix},\quad SRx=
\begin{pmatrix}
-4 \\
1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

$RSx\neq SRx$.

**Rule:** $RSx$ means: first $S$, then $R$ (read from right to left).

**Geometry:** $S$ stretches only the horizontal direction. In $RSx$ it stretches the original horizontal part of $x$; in $SRx$ the vector is rotated first, so a different part gets stretched.

**Check:** lengths differ: $|RSx|=\sqrt8$, $|SRx|=\sqrt{17}$.
