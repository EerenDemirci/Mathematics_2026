# Exercise 2

**Answer**

$$
A+B=
\begin{pmatrix}
5 & 0 \\
-1 & 8 \\
\end{pmatrix},\quad A-B=
\begin{pmatrix}
-3 & 4 \\
-1 & -2 \\
\end{pmatrix},\quad 3A-2B=
\begin{pmatrix}
-5 & 10 \\
-3 & -1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Steps:** add/subtract entry by entry. For $3A-2B$:

$$
3A=
\begin{pmatrix}
3 & 6 \\
-3 & 9 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad 2B=
\begin{pmatrix}
8 & -4 \\
0 & 10 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

then subtract entry by entry.

**Rule:** addition needs the same size, because every entry needs a partner in the same position.

**Check:**

$$
(A+B)+(A-B)=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 4 \\
-2 & 6 \\
\end{pmatrix}=2A
$$
