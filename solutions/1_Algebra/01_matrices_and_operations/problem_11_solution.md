# Exercise 11

**Steps**

$$
AB=
\begin{pmatrix}
a+c & b+d \\
c & d \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad BA=
\begin{pmatrix}
a & a+b \\
c & c+d \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

Equal entries: $a+c=a$ gives $c=0$; $b+d=a+b$ gives $d=a$.

**Answer**

$$
B=
\begin{pmatrix}
a & b \\
0 & a \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad a,b\in\mathbb{R}
$$

**Check:** for $a=2$, $b=3$:

$$
AB=BA=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 5 \\
0 & 2 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$
