# Exercise 10

**Answer**

$$
Ab_1=
\begin{pmatrix}
1-2+0 \\
0-1+3 \\
\end{pmatrix}=
\begin{pmatrix}
-1 \\
2 \\
\end{pmatrix},\quad Ab_2=
\begin{pmatrix}
2+0+0 \\
0+0+1 \\
\end{pmatrix}=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 \\
1 \\
\end{pmatrix},\quad AB=
\begin{pmatrix}
-1 & 2 \\
2 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Rule:** column $j$ of $AB$ is $A$ times column $j$ of $B$: $AB=(Ab_1\ \ Ab_2)$.

**Check:** the columns of $AB$ are exactly $Ab_1$ and $Ab_2$.
