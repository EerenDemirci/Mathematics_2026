# Exercise 4

**Answer**

$$
AB=
\begin{pmatrix}
8 & 2 \\
3 & 1 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad BA=
\begin{pmatrix}
2 & 4 \\
3 & 7 \\
\end{pmatrix},\qquad AB\neq BA
$$

**Steps:** row times column, e.g. $(AB)_{11}=1\cdot2+2\cdot3=8$, $(BA)_{22}=3\cdot2+1\cdot1=7$.

**Rule:** noncommutativity: for matrices the order matters, in general $AB\neq BA$.

**Check:** $\det(AB)=8-6=2$ and $\det(BA)=14-12=2$, both equal $\det A\cdot\det B=1\cdot2$.
