# Exercise 7

**Answer**

$$
AI=IA=A,\quad A+0=A,\quad A^2=
\begin{pmatrix}
4 & 4 \\
0 & 4 \\
\end{pmatrix},\quad A^3=
\begin{pmatrix}
8 & 12 \\
0 & 8 \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Rule:** $I$ works like the number $1$ for multiplication, $0$ works like the number $0$ for addition.

**Pattern:**

$$
A^n=
\begin{pmatrix}
2^n & n2^{n-1} \\
0 & 2^n \\
\end{pmatrix}
$$

**Check:** $n=3$: $2^3=8$ and $3\cdot2^2=12$.
